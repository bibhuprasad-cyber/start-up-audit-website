WHO IT'S FOR

Built for Every Business Owner Who Needs a Clear, Outside View

An audit works best when the problem is real and the owner wants evidence, not opinions. Here is how to tell if it fits.

A good fit if

  • You run a startup, a growing business or an established one and growth has stalled or become inconsistent.
  • Your team is busy with daily operations and cannot easily see its own workflow gaps.
  • You want evidence-based priorities, not a generic checklist or a large upfront commitment.

Not the right fit if

  • You want guaranteed revenue or results. We find and prioritise real problems, and outcomes depend on how you act on them.
  • The main issue is the core product or service itself. We work on how the business is run around it: workflow, customer journey, pricing, marketing and strategy.
  • You want a one-off website, logo or ad campaign. This is an audit and strategy engagement, not a single deliverable. We do not recommend or build anything before we have found the problems.

QUESTIONS

Before You Book

How long does an audit take?

It depends on scope: the size of the business, how many of the six areas are reviewed, and how many locations or workflows are involved. We agree a timeline with you after the first conversation, before the audit begins.

How much does an audit cost?

Fees are scope-based rather than fixed packages. After the first conversation we quote for the work that is actually needed, so you do not pay for areas that do not apply to your business.

What happens after I book?

You receive a confirmation call, then we arrange a walkthrough of how your business runs, on site where that is relevant. The audit follows, and it ends with your Solution Blueprint.

What do you need from us?

Time for a walkthrough, access to the people who run the day-to-day work, and the records you are comfortable sharing. Everything you share is covered by our Confidentiality Commitment.

Do you guarantee results?

No. We identify real, evidenced problems and recommend practical solutions. Outcomes depend on implementation, market conditions and factors outside our control, and every recommendation is optional.

Do you also carry out the changes?

We can support agreed implementation within the engagement scope, working with specialists where needed. You are equally free to take the Solution Blueprint and carry it out yourself or with your own team.

OUR APPROACH

A Process Built Around Your Business

Every business is different. We adapt the audit to the problems identified, the scope required, and the resources available.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Understand how the business works through walkthroughs, records and direct observation of daily operations.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    Identify problems and opportunities, from workflow gaps and margin leaks to missed customers.

  3. 03

    Prioritise

    Analyse root causes and rank issues by business impact, risk and effort, to start where it matters.

  4. 04

    Recommend

    Develop practical solutions that fit your needs, resources, timelines and costs.

  5. 05

    Improve

    Support agreed implementation and evaluate improvements within the engagement scope.
