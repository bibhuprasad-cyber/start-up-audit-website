Sip and Squeeze

From a high-footfall stretch to a shop people can actually see: the problem, the risk, the solution and the outcome.

Ameerpet, Balkampet Road, HyderabadJuice | Lassi | Shakes
THE PROBLEM

Footfall Is Not the Issue, Visibility Is

People walk along this stretch all day. The question is whether they can see the shop. Three vantage points (southbound right side, southbound left side and the north view) were studied for pedestrian flow, sightlines and the visual noise around the shop.

Footfall on this stretch
High
Visibility of the shop
Low

The main pedestrian flow moves on the opposite side and beyond the shop's natural sightline. A divider and greenery block the cross-road view, so the shop is almost invisible from the road. The footfall is real, but it is not converting into visibility.

ROOT CAUSE

Why the Existing Signboard Does Not Work

The signboard contains information, but it does not communicate at the distance and angle at which customers actually meet the shop.

01

Brand name

The name is not clearly readable from a distance.

02

Height

The board sits around chest height, so customers must look down.

03

Menu position

Products and menu are placed even lower than the board.

04

Visual clutter

Photos, categories and decorative visuals all compete at once.

05

Distance view

From far away, everything visually blends together.

06

Purpose

A signboard's job is attention and identification, not a full menu.

THE AUDIT

Six Problems Found

Some are what customers see and feel. Others are what the owner cannot see.

01

Low visibility

The shop is not properly visible from the road, and the view from the opposite side is blocked.

02

Weak advertising

No strong visual hook in the signage, no directional cue, and the advertising potential of the front space is unused.

03

No sitting arrangement

There is no seating, so customers have no reason to stop, sit and stay.

04

Cluttered storefront

A dustbin and bike parking occupy the visual frontage area.

05

Hero product margin

Pricing was not built from cost and margin per item is not determined, so high-margin products get no priority.

06

Profit, loss and cash flow

Actual profit and loss cannot be seen clearly, and cash flow is not healthy.

THE RISK

What Happens If These Stay Unfixed

01

Low visibility

Nobody can see the shop. People walk past without noticing it, and footfall flows to competitors.

02

Weak experience

With no seating and a cluttered front, customers have little reason to stay, so they are not retained and do not come back.

03

Margin not set per product

The hero product's real earning is unknown, so the wrong products get the attention.

04

Profit and loss unclear

Actual profit or loss cannot be known, so cash flow stays unhealthy and decisions are blind.

THE SOLUTION

Problem to Solution, One by One

Each fix works with the location the shop already has. A concept demo of the new signboard followed the same logic: a clean identity, a bold wordmark readable from a distance, one hero product image, a short category line (Lassi | Juices | Shakes) and two colourways tested for daylight contrast.

  • 01Low visibility

    Use the existing footfall: add directional signage toward the dominant traffic side and pre-approach signage before customers reach the shop. No relocation needed.

  • 02Weak advertising

    Replace the cluttered board with a clean, high-contrast, distance-readable storefront sign and one clear visual hook.

  • 03No sitting arrangement

    Turn the unused front space into simple customer seating.

  • 04Cluttered storefront

    Clear the frontage: move the dustbin and bike parking out of the shop's sightline.

  • 05Hero product margin

    Work out cost and margin for each item, set prices from cost (not from competitors), and give priority to high-margin hero products.

  • 06Profit, loss, cash flow

    Track daily sales and expenses in a simple format so actual profit and loss and cash flow become visible.

THE OUTCOME

What the Owner Gains

01

More people notice the shop

Clear, high-contrast signage makes the shop visible to footfall that already passes by.

02

Brand is recognised from afar

A clean wordmark and one hero visual are readable at walking distance.

03

Better use of existing footfall

No relocation and no big spend on a new site; the current location starts working harder.

04

A better customer experience

Seating and a clean front give customers a reason to stop, stay and return.

05

Clear margin on every item

The owner knows what each product really earns and can push the high-margin ones.

06

Real profit and cash flow clarity

Actual profit and loss and cash flow become visible, so decisions stop being guesses.

SUMMARY

The Whole Case in Four Steps

01

Problem

High footfall, low visibility. Plus no seating, a cluttered front, unclear margins and unclear profit, loss and cash flow.

02

Risk

Nobody notices the shop, customers do not return, and the owner cannot see real profit or cash flow.

03

Way of solution

Clear signage, a clean frontage with seating, margin per item and simple daily tracking.

04

Outcome

More people notice the shop, a better experience, clear margins and real financial clarity.

The location stays the same. Visibility, experience and clarity change. Start-up Audit removes a business's limitations; final sales depend on the shop's own service capacity and how well the plan is executed.
